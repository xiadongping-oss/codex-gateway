Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE ORIGINAL premium desktop application icon for "Codex Gateway", but do NOT borrow visual motifs from Codex, ChatGPT or WorkBuddy. No references. Fresh independent art direction.
Brand idea: a local AI gateway that smoothly connects incoming requests to the right services. Express controlled flow, connection and dependable routing through a unique abstract sculptural form, not a literal gate or network diagram.
The mark: one bold asymmetric folded ribbon loop, made from TWO broad interleaving satin bands. The upper band is luminous warm pearl-white, the lower turning band is pale mineral jade. Together they make a compact flowing angular loop with a softly chamfered diamond-shaped center and an elegant single open seam on the lower-right. Think a beautifully engineered folded object, almost a small architectural sculpture: distinctive silhouette, few broad planes, exact smooth joins, a strong sense of flow through the form. It should feel like an ownable product brand, not a stock chain-link icon. Keep the composition dense and balanced, with no thin outlines or tiny details. Not a ring around a star, not a generic infinity symbol, not a cloud, not a flower, not a swirl of many petals.
Background: full-size rounded-square tile, rich graphite-purple #282334 with a restrained plum undertone, no bright gradient. Tile fills 98% of square canvas with extremely narrow outer transparent safety margin. The large pearl/jade emblem fills 82% of tile width AND height so it remains visibly large in a Windows taskbar. Strong light-on-dark contrast. Medium rounded corners appropriate for a professional Windows application.
Style: sophisticated contemporary product identity, carefully sculpted 2.5D satin ceramic and frosted mineral material. Minimal, beautifully proportioned, understated shallow depth, soft restrained contact shadows inside the tile. No shiny chrome, no neon glow, no cartoon or toy finish. Straight-on front view, centered, no perspective tilt. Limited palette: deep graphite-plum, pearl-white, pale mineral mint. The pale mint accent occupies about one third of the mark. Subtle lavender reflected light can appear in the pearl but must stay neutral.
Production: 1024x1024 square. One finished icon only. Entire rounded tile fully opaque; actual transparent alpha ONLY outside rounded corners. Silhouette must be bold and instantly legible at 24 and 32px. No large outer margin or cast shadow outside tile.
Strict exclusions: NO cloud/rosette, NO Codex imitation, NO OpenAI knot, NO cat/ears/mascot, NO doorway/arch/portal, NO code brackets or > < _, NO terminal prompt, NO text, NO letters, NO initials, NO badge, NO sparkle, NO external mockup, NO contact sheet, NO decorative circuit lines, NO watermark.

Tool: built-in ImageGen. No reference images used; independent design.
